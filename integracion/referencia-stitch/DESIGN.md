## Brand & Style

This design system channels the electric, tactile energy of a modern Mexican *puesto de fruta* and neighborhood market stalls into an elevated digital medium. It deliberately rejects sterile SaaS uniformity and cliché folk tropes in favor of an urban, street-smart vernacular: razor-sharp layouts, sculptural display typography, punchy hand-painted signage references (*rótulos*), and saturated botanical fruit tones.

The brand persona is warm, witty, unapologetically vibrant, and grounded in community freshness. The visual rhythm mixes disciplined editorial spacing with spontaneous street-level artifacts: graphic jagged ticket stamps, fruit crate numbering, circular price stickers, and dashed grocery voucher lines. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Contemporary Street Mercado: sun-drenched warm surfaces, tactile high-contrast borders, playful geometric badges, and deliberate typography pairings that feel savory, authentic, and bursting with life.

## Colors

The palette directly references fresh produce, market awnings, and hand-painted neighborhood stall signage:

- **Primary (`#477A45` - Verde Palma/Limón Agrio):** The anchor hue representing fresh leaves, limes, and verdant botanical richness. Used for dominant action buttons, category headers, and grounded UI structures.
- **Secondary (`#D94F70` - Rosa Mexicano):** High-energy, sharp magenta tone inspired by pitaya, shaved ice syrups, and vibrant market stall vinyls. Used for focal CTAs, discount highlights, and interactive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#E97832` - Naranja Mandarina):** Warm, citrus-infused energy used for secondary alerts, notifications, and spicy highlights.
- **Accent Yellow (`#E8B93F` - Amarillo Maíz):** Sun-drenched corn and mango warmth, perfect for badges, price tag stickers, and stars.
- **Accent Red (`#B83B32` - Rojo Chile Picante):** Bold tactile accent for warnings, spicy flavor tags, and dynamic accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFF4DF` - Crema Papel de Estraza):** A warm, comforting parchment base that softens digital glare and simulates clean food packaging paper.
- **Neutral Dark (`#29231F` - Tinta Carbón / Molcajete):** The primary text color for light mode, providing high contrast and grounding clarity without the harshness of pure black.

In dark mode, the canvas shifts to deep cacao tones (`#211D1A`) with surface containers elevated in `#302923`, illuminated by `#FFF4DF` text and full-intensity saturated accents.

## Typography

Typography balances the art of Mexican hand-lettered storefront signage (*rótulo*) with readable, modern digital pacing:

- **Headlines & Display (`Syne`):** Features wide glyphs, daring curves, and expressive geometric construction. Used in uppercase or heavy titles to channel market stall signposts, blackboard chalkboard specials, and fruit crate typography. Tighter line-heights enhance physical print punch.
- **Body & Functional UI (`DM Sans`):** Clean, geometric, geometric-grotesque sans-serif with generous apertures. Provides unencumbered legibility for product descriptions, nutritional facts, ingredient lists, and transactional UI.
- **Market Labels (`label-rotulo`):** Set in uppercase `Syne` with expanded letter-spacing to render tactile street food badges, stamp stickers (e.g., *"CHILITO Y LIMÓN"*, *"DE TEMPORADA"*), and tab navigation.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop and a 4-column responsive grid on mobile, framed by warm generous margins that mimic craft food wrappers.

- **Rhythm & Grid:** Content spans modular blocks (cards, crates, and strips) separated by clear structural gutters (`1.25rem` mobile, `2rem` desktop).
- **Asymmetry & Overlaps:** To prevent digital rigidity, contextual badges, circular stickers, and fruit callouts may deliberately break grid boundaries by negative margins (`-0.5rem` to `-1rem`), evoking adhesive price labels slapped onto fruit displays.
- **Dashed Structural Dividers:** Functional divisions (such as cart totals, recipe steps, or category sections) use 2px dashed borders (`#29231F` with 30% opacity) mimicking perforated receipt paper and market tickets.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on airy SaaS diffuse drop shadows, this design system celebrates physical, poster-like materiality and paper-goods layering:

- **Hard Contrast Shift ("Sombra de Toldo"):** Primary interactive elements and selected cards cast crisp, high-density offset shadows without blur: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #29231F`. In dark mode, this shifts to `3px 3px 0px #E8B93F` or tone-on-tone rich blacks.
- **Tonal Canvas Stacking:** Secondary depth relies on layering warm surface tiers. White or tinted pastel cream surfaces (`#FFF9EE`) stack over base `#FFF4DF` without blur, maintaining organic paper tactile quality.
- **Tactile Borders:** Containers utilize solid 1.5px to 2px crisp outlines in `#29231F` to preserve bold definition, street sign clarity, and graphic impact.

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `2`, the core UI elements utilize balanced `0.5rem` (8px) borders, scaling to `1rem` (16px) for large containers and cards. 

To evoke street vendor vernacular, shape variants are strictly defined:
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Used for ingredient chips, spicy meter indicators, and small CTA pill tags.
- **Sticker Badges (Circular & Serrated):** Circular roundels (`aspect-ratio: 1/1`) tilted at subtle micro-angles (-2° to +3°) for pricing, artisanal stamps, and limited edition drops.
- **Perforated / Coupon Edges:** Used on delivery vouchers and coupon blocks with scalloped circular cutouts at edge seams.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#477A45` (or `#D94F70` for high-impact promos) background, text in `#FFF4DF`, 2px solid `#29231F` border, `0.5rem` border radius, and a crisp offset shadow (`3px 3px 0px #29231F`). On hover, translate by `1px 1px` with reduced shadow.
- **Secondary Action:** Cream background (`#FFF4DF`), 2px solid `#29231F`, bold typography in `#29231F`.
- **Text Link Button:** Underlined with a thick, playful wavy or 2px solid accent underline in `#D94F70`.

### Cards (Product & Market Displays)
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF8`, encircled with a 2px `#29231F` border, `1rem` corner radius, and solid 4px offset shadow.
- Floating badge in top corner for dietary and flavor profiles (*"Con Tajín"*, *"100% Fresco"*).
- Internal layout cleanly divided by subtle dashed horizontal lines.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules with `9999px` radius, styled with vibrant fills (`#E8B93F`, `#D94F70`, `#477A45`) paired with high-contrast `#29231F` labels set in `label-rotulo`.
- Interactive filter chips turn solid `#29231F` with `#FFF4DF` text when active.

### Lists & Menu Tickers
- Market-inspired line items with title on the left, dotted leader line bridging the gap, and bold price tag on the right.
- Hover states tint the row with a light splash of `#FFF4DF` or `#E8B93F` at 20% opacity.

### Form Inputs
- Background in clean off-white `#FFFDF9`, border 2px solid `#29231F`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state expands to a vibrant 2px outline in `#477A45` with a subtle offset drop-color.
- Placeholder text in `#29231F` at 50% opacity in `DM Sans`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square checkboxes (`0.25rem` radius) and circular radio buttons with thick 2px `#29231F` frames.
- Checked state fills with `#477A45` with a cream checkmark icon or solid center pip.

### Snack & Flavor Badges ("Sellos Callejeros")
- Octagonal and circular warning/accolade badges parodying local food labeling (e.g., *"EXCESO DE SABOR"*, *"CON TODO"*), rendered in high-contrast black and yellow/red blocks with uppercase `Syne` lettering.
